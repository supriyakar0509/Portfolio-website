// About me

Mathematics meets the real world

I'm Supriya, a mathematician and data scientist based in Assam, India. My foundation is a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Mathematics from Gauhati University, and I'm currently building toward an M.Sc. in Mathematics alongside deep work in applied finance and machine learning.

My work sits at the intersection of rigorous quantitative thinking and practical technology — whether that's building predictive models for financial data, designing AI-accelerated websites, or developing fraud detection systems.
